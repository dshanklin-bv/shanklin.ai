Stretchgood is a planned Fort Worth, Texas effort to give free stretch-limo rides to people who need them. It is not running yet. Nothing here is a claim that we already have nonprofit status, a partner agreement, a purchased car, or a single ride on the road.

A community that shows up

Stretchgood is a Fort Worth effort to keep a simple promise: when someone has a big night, the community shows up. We do that by giving free stretch-limo rides, with no fares, no tips, and no gas money, starting with prom.

The mission is about preserving a part of ordinary life that mattered to so many of us when we were younger: the big nights that mark growing up, the feeling of being celebrated, a community showing up for its kids, the kind of night you remember for life. Not a patriotic speech. Just that part of life, kept available for people who might otherwise miss it.

We plan to start with prom, then later offer the same kind of ride to hospice patients, veterans, and wish-style experiences. Stretchgood has no affiliation with Make-A-Wish or any other wish-granting organization. The short essay behind the mission is on the Why page.

How the work is supposed to work

A good small nonprofit can say, in plain language, who it is for, what it does, and what it hopes will change. Here is that chain for Stretchgood, as a plan, not as a result we have already measured:

  • Who: people in Fort Worth who might miss a landmark night, beginning with prom kids who cannot otherwise get that ride.
  • What we do: a free, careful stretch-limo ride, arranged with a local partner, with no cost to the passenger.
  • What should happen that night: they feel celebrated. A community showed up for them.
  • What we hope lasts: they keep a memory of being worth the trouble, and Fort Worth keeps the habit of making those nights possible.
  • How we'll know: we'll count the rides, publish what each one cost, and, with permission, let people tell us whether the night felt the way we hoped. We will not pretend a count of rides is the same thing as a changed life.

If we change the mission wording, we'll change it here, in the open.
