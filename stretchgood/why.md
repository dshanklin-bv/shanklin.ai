# Why · Stretchgood

> **V1 · figures are estimates.**

*Stretchgood · 02 / 08 · Planned · Fort Worth, Texas · Not operating yet*

Stretchgood is a plan, not a running service. It is not giving rides, has no established 501(c)(3) status, and has no partner commitments. Nothing here can be booked or donated to, and no ride is promised.

This is the why behind our mission: Stretchgood is a Fort Worth effort to keep a simple promise. When someone has a big night, the community shows up.

There is a part of growing up that does not show up in a slogan. It is the night you got dressed up. Someone came to the door. The car was longer than it had any right to be, and for a little while the world arranged itself around you. You felt celebrated. Not because you had earned a prize, but because the people around you decided the night should matter.

A lot of us had a night like that, or we watched a friend have one, or we spent years wishing we had. It is easy to treat those nights as decoration. We don't. We think they are one of the ways a place tells its kids, and later its veterans and its families in hard seasons, that they are worth showing up for. If we are not careful, that part of life becomes something only some people get. The rest are told, kindly or not, that the big night is not for them.

Stretchgood is our attempt to keep that from happening here, in a small and concrete way. A stretch limo is a little ridiculous, and that is part of the point. It is ceremony. It says: this night is different. We want to give that ride to prom kids who might otherwise miss it, completely free. No fares, no tips, no gas money. If we can do that well, we want to offer the same kind of night to hospice patients, to veterans, and as wish-style experiences.

This is a plan, not a service already on the road. We would rather say so than dress it up. The work, if we do it right, will be quiet and local: one car, one partner, one season, and a community that decided to show up.
