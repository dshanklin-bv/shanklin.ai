Follow along

Watch it come together.

Planned · Fort Worth, Texas · Not operating yet. Stretchgood is a plan, not a running service. It is not giving rides, has no established 501(c)(3) status, and has no partner commitments. Nothing here can be booked or donated to, and no ride is promised.

Highlighted figures and rules are marked like thisNeeds Daniel's check until they are verified.

Stretchgood is still a plan. The most useful thing you can do right now is follow this page, and tell us if you have a skill, a space, or a gift that would help us do the first season well.

We are not asking for tax-deductible donations today, because we do not yet have a fiscal sponsor or a granted 501(c)(3) status. If you want to give money later, we will say so here once there is a proper way to receive it.

Follow the plan

This site is the record. The Plan, the Financials, and the Open decisions will change as we learn things. If you care about this, start there.

Ways people can help, when we are ready

  • A safe place to keep a long car, covered if possible.
  • A mechanic who will inspect a used stretch limo carefully, or a detailer who would help us keep it looking like a special night.
  • Fuel, tires, or other in-kind help, once we have a car.
  • A sound used stretch Town Car, donated or offered at a fair price, with a title we can verify. We will still inspect it.
  • Help with the unglamorous work: insurance shopping, bookkeeping, or walking us through fiscal sponsorship or a clean nonprofit filing.
  • Introductions, not pressure, to a local prom-dress charity, a civic group, or a business that might underwrite a season. We will not name a partner until there is an agreement.
  • Time, later, once we have decided who drives and have a safeguarding plan for minors. Wanting to help is welcome. Driving is a decision we have not made, and it will not be casual.

If you are a family hoping for a ride in spring 2027, thank you for being patient. We will say when we are actually taking requests. We would rather you hear that from this page than from a promise made too early.

What we are not doing

We are not selling rides, taking tips, or asking passengers for gas money. We are not claiming that Stretchgood is already a charity, or that it is already on the road. And we are not going to fill this page with a waiting list, a countdown, or a donation button that would overstate where we are.

If you want to reach us about Stretchgood, use the contact already published on shanklin.ai, and say what you have in mind. We will keep the conversation, and the credit, in the open as much as we can.
