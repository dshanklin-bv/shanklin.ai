The same week, a Lenovo purchase of $443.81 came back with $13.31 cashback.

The 3% is not automatic. Paid plans on The X Subscription, and legacy Premium or Premium+, earn up to 3% on eligible purchases. Everyone else starts at 1% and can get 3% by receiving $1,000 or more in qualifying deposits within a trailing 34-day period: ACH direct deposits, or eligible X Creator payouts. A regular debit-card load does not count.

What the first month paid

His activity also shows an "X Welcome Gift" of $25.00, a $600.00 deposit from a Visa debit, and a November 2026 interest payout of $0.34. The account showed 6.00% APY. I am leaving the exact balance out of this post.

The $25.00 gift is on the ledger. I could not find a current official page that publishes a standing $25 welcome offer or its rules. Public reports of welcome amounts have varied: $25 in some signup writeups, $15 in others, $5 in an October 2026 Forbes piece. I am treating the $25 as what posted to this account, not as a promise that every new account gets the same credit.

The 6.00% APY is variable. Official rates, effective October 12, 2026, pay it to subscribers on Premium, Super, or Ultra, and to legacy Premium+. Other users earn 4.00%, or 6.00% with the same qualifying-deposit test. New York residents earn no APY. At 6.00% APY, $200 left in the account for a full year would earn about $12, if the rate and the balance both held. The $13.31 cashback is already worth more than a year of that interest.

What changes on December 1

The rate can change at any time. Starting December 1, 2026, 6.00% APY applies only to the first $150,000; amounts above that earn 4.00%. The same date also cuts ATM operator-fee reimbursements for paid subscribers to a cap of $4 per transaction, two per calendar month, according to the official FAQ. X Payments LLC is not a bank. Funds are held at Cross River Bank, Member FDIC, with pass-through coverage up to $250,000. None of this is advice to open an account.
