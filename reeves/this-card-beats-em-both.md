## $13.31 is 3%

The same week, his X Money activity showed why the 3% line is not just a slogan. A Lenovo charge for $443.81, labeled General Merchandise, came back with $13.31 cashback. $443.81 times 3% is $13.3143, which rounds down to $13.31. Official [Cashback Rewards Terms](https://money.x.com/en/i/cashback-rewards-terms) allow rounding down to the nearest cent, and rewards credit every seven calendar days after a purchase settles. Lenovo as general merchandise is not on the excluded merchant-category list. The card is a Visa debit issued by Cross River Bank, which matches the engraving.

The 3% is not automatic for every account. Paid plans on The X Subscription, and legacy Premium or Premium+, earn up to 3% on eligible purchases. Everyone else starts at 1% and can get the boost by receiving $1,000 or more in qualifying deposits within a trailing 34-day period: ACH direct deposits coded PPD, or eligible X Creator payouts. A regular debit-card load is not automatically a qualifying deposit. The terms also say the percentage can differ by category, that the total may be capped, and that X Payments can decide which percentage applies.

## The $25 and the $0.34

His activity also shows an "X Welcome Gift" of $25.00, a $600.00 deposit from a Visa debit, and a November 2026 interest payout of $0.34. The account showed 6.00% APY. I am leaving the exact balance out of this post.

The $25.00 gift is on the ledger. I could not find a current official page that publishes a standing $25 welcome offer or its rules. Public reports of welcome amounts have varied: $25 in some signup writeups, $15 in others, $5 in an October 2026 [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/antoniopequenoiv/2026/10/19/x-doles-out-5-welcome-gift-for-x-money-feature-but-users-are-skeptical/) piece. I am treating the $25 as what posted to this account, not as a promise that every new account gets the same credit.

The $0.34 payout is the small-balance story. Official [rates](https://money.x.com/en/i/interest-faq), effective October 12, 2026 and variable, pay 6.00% APY to subscribers on the Premium, Super, or Ultra plan, and to legacy Premium+. Other users earn 4.00%, or 6.00% with the same qualifying-deposit test. Interest is calculated on the daily balance, compounds daily, and is credited on the first of the following month. New York residents earn no APY. At 6.00% APY, $200 left in the account for a full year would earn about $12, if the rate and the balance both held. The $13.31 cashback is already worth more than a year of that interest.

## What can move

The rate is variable and can change at any time. Starting December 1, 2026, 6.00% APY applies only to the first $150,000; amounts above that earn 4.00%. The same date also cuts ATM operator-fee reimbursements for paid subscribers to a cap of $4 per transaction, two per calendar month, according to the official [FAQ](https://money.x.com/en/i/faq). X Payments LLC is not a bank. Funds are held at Cross River Bank, Member FDIC, with pass-through coverage up to $250,000 at that bank and, subject to [conditions](https://money.x.com/en/i/fdic-conditions), up to $10 million across a cash-sweep network. None of this is advice to open an account. It is one unboxing and one ledger, checked against the current terms.
